# HUD layout and panel integration

This folder contains the screen-space HUD shell for issue #17. The shell owns the screen layout and stable insertion
points; each visualization panel should be developed as a separate prefab and added to its matching slot.

## What exists

`SimHUD.prefab` is a Screen Space Overlay Canvas with a Canvas Scaler configured for a 1920x1080 reference resolution.
The layout uses normalized RectTransform anchors and scales with screen size. The main 3D camera keeps its full
viewport; the HUD panels are translucent overlays.

| Object | Position | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `LidarSlot` | Top left | LiDAR panel (`Panels/LidarPanel.prefab`, see below) |
| `MinimapSlot` | Bottom left | Minimap panel (`Panels/MinimapPanel.prefab`, see below) |
| `CameraSlot` | Right half | Camera panel (`Panels/CameraPanel.prefab`, see below) |
| Centre-left area | Between the left slots and `CameraSlot` | Unobstructed 3D view |
| `StatusBar` | Bottom edge | ROS connection, grid-step, safety, and health status |

Slot anchors are relative to the Canvas, with `(0, 0)` at the bottom-left and `(1, 1)` at the top-right. The slot
names are part of the integration contract: keep them unchanged. Each slot currently contains a translucent background
and a placeholder label, not a finished visualization.

The HUD does not create or render the planned-path overlay. A path visualizer should render in the 3D view, and its
visibility should be checked when panels are added.

## Adding a panel prefab

1. Create a separate panel prefab under `Assets/DeliveryBot/UI/Panels/`, for example `LidarPanel.prefab`.
2. Make its root a `RectTransform`. Build the panel's visual content as children of that root.
3. Set the panel root's anchors to stretch across its parent slot (`anchorMin = (0, 0)`, `anchorMax = (1, 1)`) and
   start with zero offsets. Add padding inside the panel rather than changing the HUD slot's anchors.
4. Open `SimHUD.prefab` in Prefab Mode and drag the panel prefab into the matching slot in the Hierarchy:
   - LiDAR panel into `LidarSlot`
   - Minimap panel into `MinimapSlot`
   - Camera panel into `CameraSlot`
5. Save the HUD prefab. The instance in `CitySim.unity` uses this prefab, so the layout remains separate from the
   contents of each panel.

The HUD builder nests `Panels/LidarPanel.prefab` into `LidarSlot`, `Panels/MinimapPanel.prefab` into `MinimapSlot` and
`Panels/CameraPanel.prefab` into `CameraSlot` automatically (see `NestPanel` in `SimHudPrefabBuilder`), so rebuilding the HUD keeps them. Other panels can be added
to the builder the same way, or integrated manually as nested prefab instances until then. Shared helpers live in
`Scripts/HudPanelUtil.cs` (runtime) and `Editor/HudBuilderUtil.cs` (prefab builders).

## Camera panel

`Panels/CameraPanel.prefab` (issues #19, #41) is generated by **Tools > Delivery Bot > Build Camera Panel**
(`Editor/CameraPanelPrefabBuilder.cs`). Change the layout there, not in the prefab. The nested instance in
`SimHUD.prefab` picks up a rebuilt panel automatically.

| View | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Stereo | Left and right stereo feeds side by side, each labelled |
| Stereo L / Stereo R | One stereo feed (forward-facing), larger |
| Disparity | `/stereo/disparity` (32FC1 from delivery_bot_core#10) in false colour |
| Side | Left side camera, left stereo feed, right side camera, each labelled, with optional detection markers |

- Switch views with the buttons or the `V` key. The panel creates an `EventSystem` if the scene has none.
- The feeds are the `RenderTexture`s of `StereoCameraPublisher` (no ROS round trip). They update at the capture rate
  and keep their aspect ratio (letterboxed). The caption shows the image size and the measured rate.
- Disparity is uploaded as an `RFloat` texture and coloured on the GPU (`Resources/DeliveryBot/DisparityColormap.shader`,
  turbo map over `0..maxDisparity`, default 96 px). NaN, Inf and values ≤ 0 are black. Until data arrives, the caption
  says it is waiting for the topic.
- Without stereo cameras in the scene, the panel renders a stand-in camera (`standInCamera`, default `Camera.main`) at
  10 Hz and says so in its title and status.
- Cost: no per-frame allocations or per-pixel CPU work. Measured frame time with and without the panel is the same
  within noise (about 3.6 ms per frame; 3.3 ms in the Side view with stand-ins vs 3.7 ms with the panel off).
- **Side cameras:** the panel shows the feeds registered in `SideCameraFeeds` (`Scripts/SideCameraFeeds.cs`).
  `SideCameraPublisher` on the robot registers both side cameras (`ISideCameraFeed`: side, `RenderTexture`, Unity
  `Camera`, `Captured` with the sim-time stamp) in `OnEnable` and unregisters them in `OnDisable`.
- **Side stand-ins:** only a fallback for scenes without a `SideCameraPublisher`. Without registered feeds, and only
  while the Side view is shown, the panel renders its own
  640×360 stand-in cameras (horizontal FOV 1.2 rad) at 10 Hz. They sit on the robot's `mono_{left,right}_camera`
  mounts, or yawed ±21.7° from the stand-in scene camera when there's no robot. The titles say "Stand-in", and the
  status line says the side cameras are missing.
- **Detections:** `/perception/side_points` (PointCloud2 in `base_link`, from delivery_bot_core#33) is projected through
  the camera that rendered each side feed and drawn as magenta diamonds (up to 12 per feed). The **Detections** button
  turns the overlay on and off. Markers disappear 1 s after the last message. Captions show the count, or say the
  topic is missing or stale.

## LiDAR panel

`Panels/LidarPanel.prefab` (issue #20) is generated by **Tools > Delivery Bot > Build LiDAR Panel**
(`Editor/LidarPanelPrefabBuilder.cs`). Change the layout there, not in the prefab.

The plot is centred on `base_link`, with forward up and left to the left. It has range rings every 2 m up to 12 m
(labelled at 6 and 12 m), the robot's outline (the URDF body box) and the lidar position. Scan points are coloured by
range (turbo, near = blue, far = red) and shifted by the measured `base_link → lidar_link` offset.

| Source | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Raw | `LidarScanPublisher` directly (no ROS round trip); `/scan` from ROS if the scene has no simulated lidar |
| Filtered | `/scan/filtered` (delivery_bot_core#9) |
| Mock | Synthetic, deterministic 10×8 m room with a pillar and an orbiting disc, plus a matching mock obstacle |

- Switch sources with the buttons or the `L` key. **Obstacles** and **Stop zone** turn the overlays on and off.
- **Tracked obstacles:** `/tracked_obstacles` (`TrackedObstacleArray` in `map`, delivery_bot_core#11). Each one is
  drawn as a circle with its radius and a velocity arrow showing 1 s of travel. `map` is the Unity world origin with
  ROS axes (#15), so the panel moves obstacles into `base_link` with the robot's pose in the scene.
- **Stop zone:** `/safety/stop_zone` (`PolygonStamped` in `base_link`, latched, from delivery_bot_core#20). The
  outline is coloured by `/safety/state`: grey without a state, green, amber if the state contains `SLOW`, red if it
  contains `STOP`.
- Sources and overlays all go through `ILaserScanSource` (`Scripts/LaserScanSources.cs`). The plot is one UI mesh
  (`LidarPlotGraphic`), rebuilt only when new data arrives.
- Cost: 0.68 ms per update with 360 returns, including the canvas rebuild, and no allocations in steady state.

## Minimap panel

`Panels/MinimapPanel.prefab` (issue #18) is generated by **Tools > Delivery Bot > Build Minimap Panel**
(`Editor/MinimapPanelPrefabBuilder.cs`). Change the layout there, not in the prefab.

The map shows the `map` frame from above with map +x to the right and +y up (like RViz). The view covers the exported
city map (−50…50 m) grown to all static colliders that reach into the obstacle band, plus 2 m padding.

| Marker | Source |
| --- | --- |
| City | Orthographic top-down camera (`Robot`, `Dynamic` and `UI` layers excluded) that renders only depth; `Resources/DeliveryBot/MinimapHeight.shader` colours it by height: street, curb (< 0.08 m), obstacle band (orange), buildings (lighter with height) and dark outlines |
| True pose (white filled arrow) | `DeliveryBotDrive.Root` (`base_link`) in the scene |
| Estimated pose (magenta outlined arrow, 1σ circle) | `/pose` (`PoseWithCovarianceStamped`, `map`, delivery_bot_core#8). The status line shows the distance to the true pose |
| Plan (cyan) | `/plan` (`nav_msgs/Path`, `map`); cleared like the 3D overlay when `/grid_step/state` is DONE or FAILED |
| Goal (green crosshair with heading) | The last goal clicked here, or the goal in `/grid_step/state` (covers goals sent from the ROS CLI) |
| Cyclists (orange discs with heading) | `DynamicObjectSpawner.Agents` in the scene |
| Costmap overlay | `/costmap/global` (`OccupancyGrid`, 0 = free … 100 = lethal, −1 = unknown; delivery_bot_core#14). Until the first costmap arrives, the latched static `/map` is shown instead. Free is transparent, costs blue → yellow, lethal red, unknown grey |

- **Click-to-goal:** click the map to publish `/goal_pose` (`PoseStamped`, `map`, stamped with sim time). Drag before
  releasing to set the heading; a plain click points the goal away from the robot. Without a ROS connection the goal
  is only logged (`[Minimap] ROS not connected, not published: …`) and still drawn.
- **Costmap (M)** toggles the overlay, **Plan** the path, **Refresh** renders the city again. The city is also
  re-rendered when `/sim/load_scenario` loads a scenario.
- Alignment: the city image, the overlay and the markers all use one `MinimapView`; the camera image matches it to
  well within one 0.05 m cell (EditMode test `MinimapGeometryTests`). The overlay is placed by the grid's origin
  (position and yaw).
- Cost: the city is rendered only on demand (start, Refresh, scenario load), and a grid is converted to a texture only
  when one arrives (2000×2000 cells: about 35 ms, once). Per frame the panel draws two textured quads and one small
  marker mesh; the GPU frame time with and without the panel was the same within noise (median 1.10 ms in both).

## Data and responsibility boundaries

Panels display simulation or ROS data; they must not implement perception, mapping, planning, or navigation logic.

| Panel or status | Suggested data/display responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| LiDAR | Visualize the scans, the tracked obstacles and the stop zone the stack publishes. Do not filter, detect obstacles, compute safety zones or plan paths in the panel. |
| Minimap | Render the Unity city and robot from above; show goal and dynamic-object markers from their existing data sources. |
| Camera | Display the stereo and side cameras' `RenderTexture`s, the disparity and the side detections from ROS. Don't compute disparity or detect objects in the panel. |
| Planned path | `PlanPathOverlay` (in CitySim, not in the HUD) draws `/plan` and the current step in the 3D view, outside the panel slots. Display only; it doesn't plan. |

`CameraSlot` itself is only the area; the camera panel inside it shows the feeds. It is not a Unity `Camera`.

## Status bar

`SimHudStatusBar` is attached to `StatusBar` and owns the four status text fields.

| Field | Current behavior |
| --- | --- |
| ROS connection | Reads `RosBridgeStatus.IsConnected` while the scene is running. |
| Grid step | Subscribes to `/grid_step/state` as `GridStepStateMsg` and displays state name, step index, replans, and yields. |
| Safety | Shows a placeholder until updated through `SetSafetyState(string)`. No topic subscription is implemented yet. |
| Stack health | Shows a placeholder until updated through `SetStackHealth(string)`. No topic subscription is implemented yet. |

When safety and health message contracts are available, add their adapters to the status component or to dedicated
components. Keep the placeholder text for the no-data state.

## Building and editing the shell

With `CitySim.unity` open, use **Tools > Delivery Bot > Build Sim HUD** to regenerate `SimHUD.prefab` and ensure it is
present in the active scene. The builder owns the Canvas, slot anchors, backgrounds, placeholder labels, and status
bar.

**Important:** the builder reconstructs the prefab from code. Running it again after manually adding panel prefab
instances can replace those additions. If the shell needs to be rebuilt, first preserve the panel prefabs separately,
then re-integrate them; alternatively, extend the builder to instantiate configured panel prefab references.

Change slot positions and Canvas settings in `Assets/DeliveryBot/Editor/SimHudPrefabBuilder.cs`, not by editing the
generated shell and then rebuilding it.

## Validation checklist

- In Unity's Game view, check **16:9**, **16:10**, and **21:9**.
- Confirm the three slots and status bar remain in their intended regions and do not overlap one another.
- Confirm the centre-left 3D area is clear and the planned path remains visible after panels are integrated.
- In Play mode, confirm the ROS connection state updates and that grid-step data replaces its placeholder when a
  message arrives.
- Confirm safety and health retain their placeholders until their inputs are connected.
